American Woman 92nd in 1970

Written by
Randy Bachman, Burton Cummings, Jim Kale, Garry Peterson
Language
English
ISWC
T-901.964.421-4 ISWC
Tags
protest, peace or political
Comments

Anti-war song in which the "American woman" refers to the Statue of Liberty, symbolizing the United States' role in drafting young men for the Vietnam War. This necessary subtlety was due to the Nixon administration's ban on overt anti-war songs.
