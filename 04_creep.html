Creep 1st in 1992

Written by
Colin Greenwood, Jonny Greenwood, Ed O'Brien, Phil Selway, Thom Yorke
Originally written by
Mike Hazlewood, Albert Hammond
Language
English
ISWC
T-071.180.847-7 ASCAP, BMI, GEMA, ISWC, JASRAC
Uses parts from
The Air That I Breathe written by Mike Hazlewood, Albert Hammond – Element
Comments

Due to similarities to "The Air That I Breathe", Albert Hammond and Mike Hazlewood are credited as co-writers of "Creep". "Creep" uses a chord progression used in "The Air That I Breathe" in its verse and a melody from "The Air That I Breathe" in the bridge following the second chorus.
