This is a representative **saved-capture test corpus**, not an exhaustive list of everything future SHS pages can contain. Arbitrary head-meta names, `data-*` attributes, URLs, free text, and dynamically generated `cyt:source-reference:*` keys have an open vocabulary and were excluded from the finite set-cover calculation. Use the raw captures and archive locators for parser assertions; live source URLs may have changed.
