Music written by
Kurt Weill

Original lyrics written by
Bertolt Brecht
Language
English

T-801.865.516-9 GEMA, ISWC
T-801.953.248-1 GEMA, ISWC
Adapted from
Alabama-Song written by Bertolt Brecht, Franz Servatius Bruinier – Recomposition
Comments

From the 1927 musical Mahagonny Songspiel , which was a kind of try-out for the longer Aufstieg und Fall der Stadt Mahagonny in 1930. Sung by the characters "Jessie" and "Bessie".
